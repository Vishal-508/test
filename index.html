How to play
  • Chor: stop on a gold ring and the job starts by itself. Roll slowly through a green beam in one of the four corners to hand the loot over. Bank the target before the clock runs out.
  • A police jeep that stays close while you are slow fills the bust meter. Keep your speed up, or lose them.
  • Hideouts (violet arrows on the road): pull up at the door and press the action button. Your car swings in and the door comes down. A search finds you if they saw you go in, or if your engine is still warm when they touch the bonnet, so get a lead before you hide. Slip out quietly or burst out.
  • Pink dashed lanes are tang galis: your small car fits, police jeeps do not. Do not sit in one; someone will call it in.
  • Hold the handbrake and steer to drift. Long drifts and close shaves refill nitro. Nitro is fast but loud.
  • The three big tijoris open when the clock tower chimes at 0:45. Their siren starts a third of the way through the job.
  • Police: stay beside a slow car to bust it. Follow alarms and tip-offs on your radar. Stop outside a hideout door and hold the action button to search it.
  • WASD or arrows to drive, Space handbrake, Shift nitro, E action, L lights, M map, hold R to get unstuck. On a phone: hold the left thumb down to drive, slide it to steer, pull back to brake.
