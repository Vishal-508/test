How to play
  • Chor: rob the glowing gold boxes, then carry the loot to a green beam in one of the four corners. Bank the target before time runs out.
  • Hide in the violet circles. Police cannot see you there unless they search that exact spot.
  • Running is fast but noisy. The three big tijoris open when the clock tower chimes at 1:00.
  • Pink dashed trails are shortcuts the police AI will not follow. Crawl pipes are for chors only.
  • Police: touch a chor to arrest. Follow the alarms on your map and search hiding spots.
  • With friends, a free chor can pull a lever beside the lock-up to free everyone inside.
  • WASD or arrows to move, Space action, Shift run, drag or Q E to turn the camera, M map. On a phone: left thumb moves, right thumb turns.
